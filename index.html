Avinash Schwarzkopf

I’m a Cognitive Science undergraduate and honors candidate at UC Berkeley, working under Steve Piantadosi at the Computation and Language Lab. My work centers on how concepts are induced and represented in learners, and how we can use these representations to understand and predict learning behavior. I’m interested in the interplay between information and its representations, including how representations emerge from observation.

Outside of my research I’m a sprint coach and writer.

Research

  • Using Finite State Machines to predict learning patterns

    Can we use a more fundamental computational measure of logical complexity to predict learning outcomes?

  • Overhypothesis formation

    Using a language of thought system to model how learners form rules-about-rules, or overhypothesis.
